Data  Fusion  utilizes  a morsel-driven  architecture  to  eliminate instances  where  high-speed  CPUs  are forced  to  wait  on  slower  disk  IO operations. This  approach effectively  bypasses  the  costly  sort exec  operation,  minimizing  memory  usage and  significantly  accelerating  data retrieval.
