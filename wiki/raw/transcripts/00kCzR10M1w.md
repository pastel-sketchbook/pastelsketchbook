As  Ryan  Solid  notes,  we  have reached  a  level  of  maturity  that  allows us  to  officially  push  the  boundaries  of front-end  development  at  its  foundation. The evolution  of  our  architecture  is centered  around  four  primary  pillars. First,  reactivity  refined  deepens  our reactive  model  by  incorporating non-nullable  async  operations  in signals,  allowing  for  more  precise  and reliable  data  management.  Second,  modern state  management  leverages  immutable diffable  stores  and  producer  patterns  to provide  a  more  predictable  state handling  environment. Finally,  we  are  prioritizing  developer experience  by  streamlining  props, refining  error  handling,  and  providing comprehensive  migration  tools  to  support developers  throughout  the  life  cycle  of their  projects. We  are  treating  async computations  as  first  class  citizens  to ensure  a  continuous  and  reliable  data flow.  By  eliminating  these  undefined states,  asynchronous  signals  become  as predictable  and  safe  as  their synchronous  counterparts.  This  refined approach  introduces  fine-grained non-nullible  async  and  robust  derived signals. With  version 2.0,  we  are  introducing  more  flexible reactive  expressions  that  can  be  updated directly  alongside  a  more  ergonomic  way to  define  computed  values. This modernization  introduces  a  more disciplined  approach  to  state  changes. By  utilizing  immutable  structures,  we make  every  update  easier  to  track,  diff, and  optimize,  which  reduces  technical overhead  and  improves  overall  system transparency.  Furthermore,  this architecture  ensures  that  large  state trees  remain  highly  performant, preventing  the  performance  bottlenecks often  associated  with  simple  reactivity.
