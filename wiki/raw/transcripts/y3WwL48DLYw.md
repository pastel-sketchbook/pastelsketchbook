Both  methods  provide critical  pathways  for  ensuring  your infrastructure  meets  application  demands effectively.  Horizontal  pod  autoscaling or  HPA  is  a  critical  mechanism  for managing  traffic  bursts  by  dynamically multiplying  application  replicas.  The process  is  initiated  by  a  trigger  based on  observed  metrics  such  as  CPU  or memory  utilization.  When  these  metrics exceed  a  specified  threshold,  the  system takes  action  by  provisioning  identical copies  of  the  application.
