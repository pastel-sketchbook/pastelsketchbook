The  evolution  of  web  transport has  reached  several  pivotal  milestones, starting  with  the  introduction  of  HTTP 1.1  in  1997. While  a  significant  step forward,  it  still  relied  on  human readable  plain  text  and  sequential pipelining  which  could  lead  to performance  bottlenecks.
