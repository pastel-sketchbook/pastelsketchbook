## Summary

This video provides a comparative analysis of two modernization strategies for migrating legacy COBOL systems to the cloud: converting to Java (leveraging enterprise-grade stability and a large developer talent pool) versus converting to Go (offering superior runtime efficiency and cloud-native fit). It evaluates both paths across porting efficiency, runtime performance, and long-term industry viability.

## Key Takeaways

- Java's object-oriented nature aligns naturally with COBOL's verbosity, enabling safer and more automated transitions with less manual rearchitecting.
- Go delivers startup times up to 100x faster than Java and significantly reduces cloud infrastructure costs, making it the clear leader in resource efficiency.
- Converting COBOL to Go often requires structural flattening and complex manual rearchitecting due to fundamental paradigm differences.
- Both paths ultimately target modern cloud architecture, but the choice depends on whether the priority is porting safety (Java) or cloud-scale operational efficiency (Go).
