# The Retirement Red Zone

## Summary

This video examines the "retirement red zone" — the critical decade spanning 5 years before and 5 years after retirement — where the transition from accumulation to decumulation phase requires a fundamentally different investment playbook. It challenges conventional target-date fund glide path wisdom using Research Affiliates' 141-year simulation showing that the classic glide path produced the lowest median ending assets ($2.1M) compared to a static 50/50 balanced approach ($2.4M) and an inverse glide path. The presentation focuses on creating resilient lifelong income while managing the paradox of needing both growth and capital preservation.

## Key Takeaways

- The "retirement red zone" is the 5 years before and 5 years after retirement, where strategies shift from wealth accumulation to capital preservation and decumulation.
- Research Affiliates' 141-year simulation showed the classic target-date fund glide path produced the lowest median ending assets ($2.1M), underperforming a static 50/50 approach by $300,000.
- The inverse glide path (starting conservative, becoming more aggressive) counterintuitively outperformed the classic approach in the simulation.
- The "retiree's dilemma" is balancing continued growth needs with the absolute necessity of preserving capital intended to fund decades of retirement.
