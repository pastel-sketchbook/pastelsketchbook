## Summary

This video provides a client-side blueprint for integrating with the ra-token-authority service to establish service-to-service trust in distributed microservice architectures. It covers the three pillars of zero trust (identity, authorization, and integrity) and details how clients authenticate, verify permissions, and ensure data integrity when communicating with other services.

## Key Takeaways

- Zero trust architecture rests on three pillars: identity (proving who a service is), authorization (verifying what it can do), and integrity (ensuring requests haven't been tampered with).
- In a zero trust environment, network location cannot be relied upon for security; every service must cryptographically prove its identity.
- The ra-token-authority provides a centralized mechanism for service-to-service authentication using cryptographically signed tokens.
- Even after authentication, each request must be checked for authorization and data integrity to defend against in-transit tampering.
