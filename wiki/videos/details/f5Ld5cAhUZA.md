## Summary

The Golden Decade Architecture targets the 10-year window from ages 65 to 75 to deploy $1.5M ($1M core + $500K 401k rolled to Roth IRA) via Secure 2.0's reverse spenddown, which eliminates RMD on 100% Roth 401ks to enable tax-free drawdowns and defuse the age-75 RMD tax bomb. ## Key Takeaways

- Secure 2.0 eliminates RMD on 100% Roth 401ks during the account holder's lifetime, enabling the reverse spenddown that replaces the old spend-taxable-last rule and avoids the age-75 RMD tax bomb.
- The dual engine preserves the $1M core indefinitely while spending down the $500K Roth booster over 120 months at $4,200/mo to generate a combined $8,040/mo ($96,500/yr) at zero federal tax.
- The 0% capital-gains haven at $98,900 married joint for 2026 keeps the $46,100 in qualified dividends from SCHD/VM tax-free when baseline income stays below the threshold.
- Roth distributions are invisible to MAGI, avoiding the $2,200 IRMA premium spike at the $218K cliff and allowing bracket topping where traditional 401k fills to the 12% bracket brim while Roth overflows outside it.
- The sweeper system routes brokerage cash (DRIP off) to a high-yield silo earning ~4% then auto-transfers $3,800/mo to checking for a smooth salary, backed by a 3-year $150K buffer that prevents selling shares in crashes and a 3.8-4.0% spending cap that reinvests the ~0.6% surplus ($6,000) to grow principal.
