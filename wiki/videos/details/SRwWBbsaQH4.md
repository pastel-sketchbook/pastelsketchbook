## Summary

A scalable property tax ingestion strategy targets all 3,143 US counties by exploiting the oligopoly of Computer Assisted Mass Appraisal (CAMA) vendors — Tyler EagleSWorld, Tyler PACS, Vision, DevNet, Ezri, Momentum, and Patriot Properties — so that 10–15 vendor adapters can capture 60–70% of national coverage.
