# Pathways Over Tools

> [development](../development.md) · 19 views · Mar 10, 2026

## Summary

This presentation argues that enterprise AI value comes from integrating individual tools into cohesive pathways rather than deploying isolated AI projects. It introduces the concept that divergent departmental needs are a feature, not a bug, and that different teams legitimately require different execution paths even when sharing the same foundational data and goals.

## Key Takeaways

- Enterprise AI initiatives should be organized as integrated pathways rather than collections of isolated tool deployments.
- Divergent departmental requirements are legitimate and should be accommodated, not dismissed as redundant duplication.
- A unified foundation that branches into specialized autonomous systems enables true enterprise-wide AI autonomy.
- The "myth of redundancy" causes organizations to incorrectly reject valid team-specific process variations as unnecessary duplication.
