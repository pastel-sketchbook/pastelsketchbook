## Summary

This guide maps the 2025–2026 annuity landscape across three core retirement objectives — guaranteed growth, protected upside, and lifetime income — and ranks leading products in each category. It compares multi-year guaranteed annuities (MYGAs), fixed indexed annuities (FIAs), registered index-linked annuities (RILAs), variable annuities (VAs), and immediate/deferred income annuities (SPIAs/DIAs) using quantitative criteria such as fees, caps, buffers, and payout rates.

## Key Takeaways

- The annuity market splits cleanly into guaranteed growth (MYGAs), protected upside (FIAs, RILAs, VAs), and lifetime income (SPIAs, DIAs), each solving a distinct retirement risk.
- Top-ranked MYGAs from Gainbridge, Athene, and New York Life deliver fee-free fixed yields up to roughly 5.80% APY for capital preservation.
- Variable annuities offer the highest growth potential of the protected-upside group and the lowest fee bands (0.10%–0.90%), led by Lincoln American Legacy, Pacific Odyssey, and Nationwide Advisory.
- SPIAs maximize payout-per-dollar for retirees aged 60–75, while DIAs trade an income-deferral period for substantially higher future income, eliminating longevity risk.
- Product selection should align the risk-return profile (floor, buffer, cap, fee) with the investor's primary objective rather than chasing a single headline yield.
