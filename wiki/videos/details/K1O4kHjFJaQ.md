## Summary

This video presents a strategic guide for young professionals covering the three essential retirement vehicles: the 401(k) as the "heavy lifter" for automated savings with tax advantages and compound interest, Social Security as "longevity insurance" providing a floor (not ceiling) for survival, and the IRA as the "flexible supplement" offering greater investment control and tax-free growth. The presentation moves from theory to strategy to application, providing a concrete road map for long-term wealth building.

## Key Takeaways

- The 401(k) is the "heavy lifter" of retirement — an employer-sponsored account that leverages compound interest, automated savings, and significant tax advantages to build the bulk of long-term wealth.
- Social Security functions as "longevity insurance" providing a base-level income floor for survival, not a ceiling for desired lifestyle — planning beyond it is essential.
- The IRA serves as the "flexible supplement" offering greater personal investment control and tax-free growth potential to fill gaps left by the other two pillars.
- Young professionals should treat this as a three-pillar system where each vehicle serves a distinct purpose rather than relying on any single account.
