## Summary

This session architects a compliant set-and-forget cross-border banking system for expatriates around an algorithmic view of account closures driven by KYC, AML, and FATCA friction engines. It establishes the survival triad of trusted residential address, real US mobile service, and stable IP usage, ranks Schwab plus Wise as the anchor hubs over Fidelity, HSBC, and neobanks, and operationalizes the Korea Standard pipeline with transfer-rail matrices, four-phase AML hygiene, transfer-versus-tax separation, and four immutable rules.

## Key Takeaways

- Treat account closures as algorithmic flagging from IP jumps, returned mail, inactivity, and virtual addresses rather than geography alone, and counter with a consistent verifiable US footprint.
- Anchor identity on Schwab checking for zero foreign transaction fees with full ATM refunds paired with Wise multicurrency for mid-market conversion and local routing numbers.
- Route transfers deliberately with Wise at 0.3 to 0.6 percent spreads for monthly flows versus Swift wires above $40 for lump sums over $250,000 needing compliance trails.
- Stage large movements in $50,000 to $100,000 chunks with source documentation, fraud-department pre-notification, and stable IPs since failures stem from compliance algorithms rather than technical errors.
- Separate transfer mechanics from tax reality by executing the US taxable distribution first with withholding and foreign-tax-credit planning before moving post-tax cash across borders.
