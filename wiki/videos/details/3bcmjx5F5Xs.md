# The 10-Year AI Investing Architecture

## Summary

This framework structures a decade-long AI investment portfolio as a three-tier barbell that balances durable infrastructure with controlled asymmetric exposure. It allocates 45–55% to a secure core (compute, cloud, power, networking), 30–40% to mid-conviction picks-and-shovels of applied AI, and 10–15% to venture-style moonshot optionality with capped downside.

## Key Takeaways

- A barbell portfolio anchored in AI infrastructure compounds value regardless of which specific models or platforms ultimately win.
- The secure core targets compute accelerators, cloud hyperscale, power and energy expansion, and networking and switching as the rails of the AI economy.
- The mid-conviction tier focuses on vertical AI platforms, enterprise workflows, security and governance, and robotics where adoption and revenue are already measurable.
- The controlled-asymmetry sleeve pursues agent ecosystems, frontier model challengers, synthetic data, and AI-native consumer apps with bounded position sizing.
- Engineering asymmetry by capping downside while preserving upside optionality is the central discipline that separates structural AI investing from hype chasing.
