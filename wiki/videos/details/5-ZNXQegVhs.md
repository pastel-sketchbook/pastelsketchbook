## Summary

This video argues for unifying shift-left (proactive prevention) and shift-right (production resilience) strategies within the SDLC rather than choosing one over the other. It quantifies the exponential cost of defects — from $100 at design to $100,000 in production — and cites that 68% of users abandon an app after just two glitches, making early quality integration a business imperative.

## Key Takeaways

- Defect remediation costs multiply 10x at each SDLC stage: $100 at design, $1,000 during coding, $10,000 at integration, and $100,000+ in production.
- 68% of users abandon an app after experiencing just two glitches, and 75% of cart abandonment is linked to poor software experience.
- Shift left integrates security and quality at the beginning of development; shift right leverages monitoring and observability in production.
- Together, these strategies create a continuous virtuous cycle depicted by the DevOps infinity loop, ensuring software that is both high-quality and resilient.
