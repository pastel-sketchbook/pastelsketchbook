# Agentic Platform Engineering with GitHub Copilot

> [kubernetes](../kubernetes.md) · 66 views · Mar 27, 2026

## Summary

This presentation by Diego Casati and Ray Cao introduces agentic platform engineering as a paradigm shift from reactive firefighting to autonomous, reasoning-capable infrastructure platforms powered by GitHub Copilot. The core thesis is that infrastructure complexity has outpaced human cognitive capacity, creating a "paradox of choice" that demands AI-augmented operations. The approach embeds an observe-reason-act loop into platform engineering workflows, enabling platforms to self-diagnose and respond to issues.

## Key Takeaways

- Modern infrastructure complexity has created a cognitive bottleneck where platform engineers cannot manually manage thousands of disparate components across cloud services and codebases.
- Agentic platform engineering embeds an AI layer with three capabilities: observation (monitoring), reasoning (analysis), and action (automated remediation).
- GitHub Copilot serves as the agentic interface, enabling platform teams to interact with infrastructure through natural language rather than memorizing tool-specific commands.
- The transition moves platform engineering from reactive incident response to proactive, self-healing infrastructure that reasons alongside the team.
