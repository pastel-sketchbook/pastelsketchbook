## Summary

This talk frames physical AI as an ecosystem problem — architecture, orchestration, and multi-robot collaboration toward a Horizon 2040 vision — rather than isolated tools. ## Key Takeaways

- The era of the solo robotic tool is over: a single arm cannot run a trillion-parameter physical model onboard; the foundational pillar of physical AI is inter-robot communication, swarm intelligence, and fleet orchestration.
- By 2040 the bottleneck is not individual intelligence but how effectively robots share spatial awareness, intent, and learned physical skills in real time as a collaborative system.
- In the physical world latency has mass: a 50ms spike that only costs a dropped frame in digital AI can mean a robotic arm dropping a fragile part or failing to stabilize a 500 lb load.
- Physical AI goals must shift from perfect code compilation to robust physical resilience in chaotic environments.
- Robotic semiconductor anatomy maps five layers — high-density compute SOCs/chiplets (brain), MPUs for OS/SLAM/AI inference (cerebrum), plus sensing, actuation, and power — into an integrated stack that enables low-power autonomous operation.
