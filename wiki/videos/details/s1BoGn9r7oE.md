## Key Takeaways

- Active ETFs employ professional managers who actively buy and sell assets to outperform benchmarks, but carry higher expense ratios than passive index-tracking funds.
- Covered call strategies require owning 100 shares and selling call options at a strike price, collecting premiums regardless of outcome but capping upside gains.
- Covered call ETFs automate the options-selling process by having professional managers systematically write call options against a basket of held stocks.
- Investors must evaluate the trade-off between receiving immediate premium income and sacrificing potential capital appreciation above the strike price.
