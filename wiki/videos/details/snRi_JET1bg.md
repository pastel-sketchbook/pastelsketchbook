# Deploying CSR Applications on Vercel Versus AKS

> [kubernetes](../kubernetes.md) · 8 views · Jan 7, 2026
> [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/snRi_JET1bg)

## Summary

This video compares two fundamentally different deployment philosophies for client-side rendered (CSR) applications: Vercel's platform-as-a-service approach versus Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) as infrastructure-as-a-service. It examines how both platforms ultimately serve static assets (HTML, JavaScript, CSS) to browsers but differ dramatically in developer experience, infrastructure control, and operational complexity. The presentation helps developers choose between speed-to-market with Vercel and granular infrastructure control with AKS.

## Key Takeaways

- CSR deployment is fundamentally about serving static assets, but Vercel optimizes specifically for this front-end workflow while AKS treats it as a generic containerized workload.
- Vercel provides a streamlined developer-centric PaaS experience with minimal configuration, ideal for teams prioritizing rapid deployment and iteration.
- AKS offers full container orchestration and granular infrastructure control but requires significantly more configuration and operational management overhead.
- The choice between platforms hinges on whether the team values a tailored front-end deployment experience or needs broader infrastructure flexibility for long-term goals.
