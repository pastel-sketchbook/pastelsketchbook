## Summary

This video teaches candlestick chart analysis as a strategic framework for reading price action psychology, going beyond pattern memorization to contextual interpretation. It covers the anatomy of individual candlesticks (open, high, low, close, body size, wick length), then builds toward a three-part framework: the toolkit (identifying building blocks), the rule book (filtering signals with logic and context), and the action plan (step-by-step trade execution). The central thesis is that a candlestick pattern's significance depends entirely on where and why it appears.

## Key Takeaways

- A candlestick pattern is only meaningful in context—the same formation signals different outcomes depending on its location relative to support, resistance, and trend.
- Long wicks reveal price rejection and indicate where one side (buyers or sellers) pushed back against the other, making them critical signals of potential reversals.
- The body size indicates momentum strength: larger bodies signal stronger directional conviction, while small bodies suggest indecision.
- The three-part framework (toolkit, rule book, action plan) structures the progression from pattern identification through signal filtering to disciplined trade execution.
