## Key Takeaways

- The framework applies three dimensions of extraction to a single source set: Synthesizers extract depth, Auditors check rigor, and Architects build actionable strategy.
- Synthesizers go beyond summarization to interpret why information matters to various stakeholders, establishing comprehensive understanding of core content.
- Auditors stress-test claims, map supporting evidence, and proactively expose hidden flaws or inconsistencies to ensure credible foundations.
- Architects transform validated insights into step-by-step blueprints and strategic frameworks, moving from theoretical knowledge to executable strategy.
