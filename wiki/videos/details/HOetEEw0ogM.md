- The 2023-2025 era of AI-as-assistant (reactive code completion) is giving way to 2026's AI-as-agent paradigm where agents independently plan, execute, and verify complex SDLC tasks.
- While task execution can be delegated to AI agents, accountability for outcomes must remain with humans — this is the foundational principle of enterprise agentic AI.
- The implementation requires phased Azure DevOps integration starting with establishing the Azure foundation before deploying autonomous agents.
- Enterprise deployment demands extreme security and precision, treating AI agents as a form of digital labor with the same governance as human workers.
