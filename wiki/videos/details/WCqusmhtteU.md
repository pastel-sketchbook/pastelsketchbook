# The Blueprint for AI-Augmented Agile

> [development](../development.md) · 32 views · Sep 20, 2026
> [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/WCqusmhtteU)

## Summary

This session lays out a practical blueprint for AI-augmented Agile that bridges domain-driven design with AI-driven execution across three concentric rings around the core domain. It assigns strategic work like business rules, invariants, ubiquitous language, bounded contexts, and aggregates to humans while AI serves as the first-pass mechanic for style, performance, complexity, security, and tests, then walks through a four-pillar ownership matrix, an augmented SDLC from event storming to retrospectives, an e-commerce order aggregate example, AI-assisted context mapping and ADRs, a dual-gate review funnel, augmented definitions of ready and done, an AI-paired workday timeline, and three non-negotiable guardrails.

## Key Takeaways

- Keep humans accountable for domain decisions including aggregates, invariants, and ubiquitous language while AI owns mechanical first-pass work and carries zero accountability.
- Run reviews through a dual-gate funnel where automated technical audits filter style, security, and N+1 issues before humans validate domain alignment and bounded-context integrity.
- Gate sprints with an augmented definition of ready covering product-owner priority, defined ubiquitous language, plain-language domain rules, AI debt assessment, and clean domain-versus-technical splits.
- Enforce the three guardrails of domain clarity over clever optimizations, a continuously updated glossary injected into every AI prompt, and human explainability for all merged AI-generated code.
- Amplify rather than replace expertise by pairing human acceptance criteria with AI-scaled unit, property, and load test generation across discovery, planning, design, implementation, review, testing, and retros.
