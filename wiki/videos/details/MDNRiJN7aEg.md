## Summary

This video outlines a high-conviction leveraged investment strategy using a 90/10 core-satellite split between QQQM (NASDAQ 100 tracker) and TQQQ (3x leveraged NASDAQ 100 ETF) to engineer a 1.2x leveraged portfolio. It covers the blueprint, potential data-driven projections, the perils including historical lost decades, and a practical toolkit for implementation and risk management. The presentation is framed as an educational guide for self-directed investors rather than a financial recommendation.

## Key Takeaways

- The strategy allocates 90% to QQQM (chosen over QQQ for its lower 0.15% expense ratio) and 10% to TQQQ (3x daily leveraged NASDAQ 100) to achieve an effective 1.2x portfolio leverage.
- TQQQ's 3x daily leverage means the 10% satellite position provides the amplified returns, but also concentrates all significant risk in that small allocation.
- Historical "lost decades" where the NASDAQ underperformed represent the greatest threat to this strategy, as leveraged ETFs suffer from volatility decay during extended sideways or declining markets.
- The expense ratio difference between QQQ and QQQM, while seemingly small, compounds significantly over a 10+ year investment horizon.
