# Nesting in Global Finance

## Summary

This video explains nesting in global finance—a layered correspondent banking arrangement where smaller respondent banks access international payment networks indirectly through intermediary banks rather than establishing direct relationships. It contrasts transparent direct correspondent banking with opaque downstream nested chains involving fintechs and money service businesses, highlighting the compliance risks and reduced visibility inherent in nested payment architectures.

## Key Takeaways

- Nesting allows smaller respondent banks to access global payment networks through an intermediary's correspondent account without a direct relationship.
- Downstream nesting creates opaque payment chains where ultimate customers transact through intermediaries (fintechs, MSBs) before reaching correspondent banks.
- Direct correspondent banking provides transparent data flow, while nested chains introduce compliance risks due to reduced visibility.
- The payment architecture in global finance is increasingly split between direct relationships and opaque nested chains.
