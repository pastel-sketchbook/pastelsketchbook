# The Evolution of RFCs in the Al Era

> [development](../development.md) · 11 views · Apr 16, 2026

## Summary

This video examines how RFCs (Request for Comments) are evolving from natural-language documents interpreted by humans into executable, mathematically verifiable specifications that machines can autonomously implement. It argues that protocols like TCP, Raft, and TLS have suffered from ambiguity and implementation drift due to manual interpretation, and that the AI era demands formal living standards that serve as literal test-driven compiler targets.

## Key Takeaways

- Traditional RFCs rely on human interpretation of natural language, leading to ambiguity, edge-case failures, and implementation drift across systems.
- The AI era requires executable, mathematically verifiable living standards rather than static descriptive documents.
- Protocols like TCP, Raft, and TLS are shifting from prose specifications to formal definitions that function as test-driven compiler targets.
- Machine-verifiable standards ensure provable correctness and consistent behavior across autonomous and distributed networks at scale.
