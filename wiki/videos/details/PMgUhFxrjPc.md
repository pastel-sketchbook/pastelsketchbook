## Summary

This video provides a systematic cross-cloud service mapping across Azure, AWS, and GCP, organized into a four-layer architecture: foundation, data, operations, and innovation. It walks through the compute continuum from IaaS through PaaS, CaaS, and Serverless, then covers storage categories, networking topology, database services, and AI/ML platform equivalents across all three providers.

## Key Takeaways

- Cross-cloud fluency requires a mental model organized by function rather than brand—the four-layer architecture (foundation, data, operations, innovation) provides that Rosetta Stone.
- The compute continuum (IaaS → PaaS → CaaS → Serverless) maps cleanly across providers, with each tier trading control for operational simplicity.
- Storage, networking, and database services have direct equivalents across Azure, AWS, and GCP, but naming and default behaviors diverge enough to create migration pitfalls.
- AI/ML platform comparison reveals convergence in capability but divergence in integration depth, with each provider optimizing for its own ecosystem.
