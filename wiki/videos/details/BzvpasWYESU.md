# The LLM Wiki Paradigm

> [development](../development.md) · 2.2K views · Apr 9, 2026
> [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/BzvpasWYESU)

## Summary

The LLM Wiki paradigm replaces traditional stateless Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with a stateful, persistent architecture where large language models act as continuous compilers of a markdown-based knowledge graph. By separating immutable raw sources from synthesized markdown files and employing a structured three-layer architecture, the system enables organizational knowledge to compound over time rather than being rediscovered for every query.

## Key Takeaways

- The architecture shifts from amnesiac stateless retrieval to a persistent system where every query result is automatically filed back into a markdown directory to facilitate future synthesis.
- A three-layer system design maintains data integrity by strictly separating immutable raw sources from the synthesized markdown wiki and the governing schema templates.
- Automated linting and divergence checks allow LLM agents to maintain knowledge health by fixing broken links, flagging stale claims, and autonomously generating counter-arguments to reduce bias.
- Spatial and temporal navigation is managed through lightweight index.md and log.md files, allowing LLMs to traverse the knowledge graph without requiring complex semantic search infrastructure at moderate scales.
- The human role shifts from repetitive data maintenance to editor-in-chief, focusing on strategic curation and high-level verification while the LLM handles intensive bookkeeping and formatting tasks.
