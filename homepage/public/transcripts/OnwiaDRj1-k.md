In  Kubernetes,  pods  are  inherently ephemeral.  They  can  be  created, destroyed,  and  replaced  at  any  time, often  receiving  a  new  IP  address  each time  they  are  rescheduled. A  service  acts  as  a  durable front  end  offering  a  stable  logical endpoint  with  a  persistent  IP  address and  DNS  name  shown  here  as  my app.default. Regardless  of  whether  pod  1,  2,  or  3  is replaced  or  scaled,  the  service  remains constant.  It  intelligently  routes incoming  traffic  to  the  currently  active group  of  back-end  pods,  ensuring seamless  connectivity  and  decoupling  the consumer  from  the  fluctuating  life  cycle of  the  underlying  infrastructure.  In Kubernetes,  there  are  four  primary service  types  used  to  manage  how applications  are  accessed  both  inside and  outside  the  cluster.
