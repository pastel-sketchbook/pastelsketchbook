Axum  stands  out  by  providing  an ergonomic,  modular,  and  type-safe approach  to  HTTP  routing.  By  leveraging the  strengths  of  the  Rust  programming language,  it  ensures  that  your  web services  are  not  only  high-performance, but  also  remarkably  reliable  and  easy  to maintain. Our  design  philosophy  centers on  three  fundamental  pillars: ergonomics,  modularity,  and  type  safety.
