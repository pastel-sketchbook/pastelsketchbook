To understand  performance  optimization,  we must  embrace  a  fundamental  mental  model. The  bottleneck  is  memory,  not  math. Modern  processors  are  incredibly efficient  at  computation,  but  they  are frequently  stalled  while  waiting  for data.  At  the  top  of  our  hierarchy,  we have  CPU  registers.  Math  performed directly  on  registers  is  faster  than even  the  L1  cache.  As  we  move  down  into the  L1,  L2,  and  L3  caches,  we  enter  what is  known  as  the  danger  zone  for eviction. This is  the  true  bottleneck  of  the  system, operating  at  an  order  of  magnitude slower  than  the  cache  layers  above  it.
