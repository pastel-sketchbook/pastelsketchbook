Building  an application  that  functions  on  localhost is  one  thing,  but  running  a  resilient, scalable,  and  secure  enterprise  grade service  under  real  world  traffic requires  deliberate  architectural hardening.  Over  the  course  when  code runs  flawlessly  on  a  developer's machine,  it  often  conceals  an  entirely different  operational  reality  awaiting it  in  production.  In  local  development, our  mental  model  assumes  simplicity.
